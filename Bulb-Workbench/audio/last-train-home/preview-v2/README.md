# Last train home — preview v2

Revision prompted by the owner's listening report that v1 rain was too loud and
the train could not be heard clearly. Version 1 remains intact in its own folder.

[Listen to v2](last-train-home-60s.mp3) or use the [WAV master](last-train-home-60s.wav).
The [receipt](provenance.json) records actual measurements and file hashes; the
revised synthesis source is saved beside it.

The same seeded sounds and timeline are used for comparison. Before master
normalization, the rain, canopy and patter gains are halved (about -6 dB); train
gain is multiplied by 2.24 (about +7 dB), brakes by 2, and air release by 1.67.
The integrated master target is reduced from -24 to -27 LUFS, so normalization
does not simply return the rain to its former overall level. Those are production
adjustments, not a claim of improved perceived realism.

- 0–4 seconds: fade in.
- 16–31 seconds: train approach builds.
- About 31–34 seconds: main train body strongest.
- 29–42 seconds: brake texture, overlapping the rolling body.
- 39–45 seconds: air release and settling away.
- 53–60 seconds: fade to silence.

This is an arrival/settling scene, with no separate departure. It remains a finite
original procedural preview without speech, music, automatic loop or IR commands.
No device was operated and the agent has not auditioned the result. The next
listening question is whether the train is recognizable and the rain comfortable
on the owner's chosen playback equipment.
