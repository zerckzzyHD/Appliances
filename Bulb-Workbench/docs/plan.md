# Bulb plan

Goal: a comfortable late-night station atmosphere for sleep, with restrained
rain, an audible distant train and slow Pink/Purple/Blue lighting. The current
[status](status.md) records owner observations, historical v5 acceptance and
the subsequent revisions and current v12 resonance-trim candidate awaiting listening.
The [imported specification](history/GE-SPEAKER-BULB-IR-PLAN-2026-10-22.md)
retains the detailed earlier scene proposal and pinned upstream source leads.

1. The owner approved the three-minute extension and requested slightly quieter
   rain again. Scene v4 is rendered with rain layers 1.5 dB below v3, unchanged
   train source/master gains, one arrival and no announcement or horn. Retain
   the approved 60-second preview unchanged.
2. The owner reports v4 mixing is much better but requests a smaller rain trim
   and more sonic detail. V5 reduces only rain-family gains another 0.75 dB.
   The owner accepted v5. Preserve that rain/train balance while adding
   restrained ambient detail. V6 adds sparse rail contacts, faint mechanical
   rattles and seven soft receding footsteps. The owner approved trying steps
   but flagged apartment-related unease; provide both with/without-step mixes
   and an isolated removable step layer. The owner then reported v6 rain masks
   the added sounds and asked for quieter rain and details near its level.
   V7 reduces rain another 1.5 dB, calibrates active clicks/rattles toward the
   surrounding bed and keeps the original train gain. The owner then rejected
   footsteps and found clicks too similar, sudden and random. V8 removes
   both scattered click/rattle families, adds shared slowing-wheel modulation
   and braking-linked envelopes during the train, and includes no footsteps.
   The owner could not confidently identify v8 additions. V9 raises those same
   coherent layers toward -3 dB versus the train-window bed and provides a
   mechanics-only 30-second identification clip at the same mix level. The owner
   heard the additions but found them overtake the train's end and authorized
   more detail. V10 trims mechanics 1.5 dB and fades them at 1:19-1:25, retaining
   the original train ending. Wind-linked airflow/frame resonance pairs add
   detail before and after the train. The owner still reported rain masking the
   new ventilation. V11 lowers rain another 1.5 dB and brings airflow/resonance
   toward +1/-2 dB active-core RMS versus the full underlying bed. Background
   measurement found continuous non-rain ventilation roughly as strong as rain,
   so that rain trim changes total wash only about 0.65 dB. Next is owner
   comparison of the full scene and exact-level station-only identification
   clip, using the [complete timeline](../audio/last-train-home/scene-v11/README.md).
   The owner found v11 resonance too loud. V12 trims only resonance 2 dB, with
   independent working airflow/resonance controls. Next is owner listening to
   v12, especially 0:22-0:35 and 2:04-2:22. A full five-family configuration for
   rain, continuous wash, airflow, train/mechanics and resonance remains future
   work; separating the wash from rain is the next useful broader mixing step.
   Original train and v10 mechanics stay unchanged; no footsteps or impacts.
   Measured RMS and common timing do not prove perceived realism.
   Longer loops and optional PA remain later choices. No overnight suitability
   is established.
3. Start lighting integration with a static, owner-selected Pink preset and
   manual audio playback. Confirm the actual speaker destination and stop
   controls during an explicitly approved owner-present test.
4. Only then assess a finite Pink/Purple/Blue macro using the installed app.
   Source inspection indicates foreground lifecycle cancellation; installed
   version and background audio continuity need confirmation. Two manual
   starts do not establish synchronization. Keep Green out of the dependency
   path. Do not assume independent addressing or smooth lamp fades.

USB/ADB, a custom app, installations, stereo re-pairing, automatic broadcasts
and unattended operation are not prerequisites. An audio asset edit does not
authorize a device operation.
